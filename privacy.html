2. What data is processed

NoteBuddy works completely offline. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no network code. The provider does not receive, store or process any of the data listed below. It stays on your device, so no processing by the provider takes place and no legal basis under Art. 6 GDPR is needed for it.

DataPurpose
Player profile (XP, language, instrument, sound, haptics and reminder settings, note-naming choice)Run the game and remember your settings
Per-note statistics (attempts, hits, answer time, repetition schedule)Choose which notes to practise next
Level progress (unlocked levels, stars, best score)Show your progress
Practice days and XP per dayStatistics, streak and reminder text

All of it is stored only on your device, until you reset your progress in the app's settings or delete the app. No name, email, account, device identifier or location is collected.

Backups made by Apple (iCloud or computer backup) may contain the app's data. They are governed by your own Apple settings and Apple's terms.

3. Permissions

Notifications: one local reminder in the evening to practise. It is scheduled on the device and may mention your streak. If you decline, there is no reminder and everything else works unchanged. You can switch it off in the app or in iOS Settings.

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, Bluetooth or the network.

5. Contact by email

If you email the provider, your address and message are processed to answer you, and are stored at the provider's email service (GMX, Germany). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (answering enquiries), or Art. 6(1)(b) if your message relates to a contract. Messages are deleted once your enquiry is settled, unless a legal duty requires keeping them longer.

6. App Store and TestFlight

The app is distributed by Apple. Apple processes data under its own privacy policy, for example download records. From the App Store, the provider sees only aggregated figures in App Store Connect.

If you test the app through TestFlight, Apple shows the provider, depending on how you joined, your name and/or email address, your installs and sessions, the feedback and screenshots you send, and crash reports. The provider uses this only to run the test and improve the app. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is testing the app. The data is deleted when it is no longer needed for the test. You can leave a test at any time in the TestFlight app.

7. Other recipients and third countries

Beyond the cases in sections 4 to 6, no data is passed on. There are no analytics, advertising networks or cloud storage run by the provider, and the app itself transfers nothing to third countries.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). The data inside the app is not held by the provider; you can erase it yourself in the app. Contact: florian_stockinger@gmx.de

9. Right to complain

You may complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live or work. The authority responsible for the provider is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), www.lda.bayern.de.
